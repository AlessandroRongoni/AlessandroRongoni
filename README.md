## About

I'm **Alessandro Rongoni**, a **Software Engineer, Web Developer and AI / Computer Vision Engineer** based in Tokyo, Japan. I enjoy building things end to end: from web and mobile applications to deep-learning pipelines, embedded systems and practical AI prototypes.

I'm also pursuing a **PhD at Tokyo University of Agriculture and Technology (TUAT)**, where I explore AI applied to real-world challenges, including computer vision, multimodal learning and smart agriculture. My background combines hands-on software development with applied research.

Beyond engineering and research, I explore **indie games, pixel art and creative coding** — especially where technical systems meet interactive design.
